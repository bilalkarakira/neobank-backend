End of day: capture and clearing

Until the day is closed an approval only reserved the money. Closing the day puts every approved payment (with its final amount, for example a tip) and every refund into one clearing file for the network, which passes it to the card's bank. Voided or declined payments are not in it.

Chargebacks

When a cardholder disputes a payment, their bank files a chargeback and the network passes it to us. The money has already been taken back at settlement. If you have proof the sale was fine (a signed receipt, a delivery note), answer it once with a note; it goes to the cardholder's bank, and if it holds the money comes back to you.
